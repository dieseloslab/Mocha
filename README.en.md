# Mocha Linux

**Built to play. Built to help.**

An Arch Linux-based distribution for gaming, productivity and everyday use, with its own identity, strong performance and graphical update and recovery tools.

## What makes Mocha different

- **Strong gaming performance:** designed to compete with Windows and other gaming distributions and able to outperform them in several games and scenarios tested on the same machine.
- **Mocha LQX kernel:** based on Liquorix and prepared for low latency, responsiveness and performance.
- **Rollback to the last valid configuration:** critical updates can be reverted when a kernel, driver or essential component causes a failure.
- **Dedicated recovery partition:** an independent environment for diagnosis, repair and restoration of the main system.
- **Complete graphical interface:** updates, maintenance, recovery and component selection without forcing users to rely on the terminal.
- **Gaming-ready foundation:** Steam, Proton, GameMode, MangoHud and project-selected drivers.

## Performance without fabricated figures

Mocha Linux is being compared on the same machine with Windows and other gaming-focused distributions. It has led in several scenarios and remained competitive in the others.

Numerical results will only be published with the hardware, versions, settings and reproducible methodology used in the test. Actual performance varies by game, driver and equipment.

## Updates and recovery

Mocha is designed to give important updates a clear return path. When a kernel, driver or essential component update causes problems, the system can return to the last validated configuration.

The recovery partition complements this mechanism with an independent graphical environment for diagnosing, repairing and restoring the main installation.

## T21 social mission

Mocha is not only a technical project. It maintains a social mission connected to **Trisomy 21**, focused on awareness, inclusion, visibility, support and responsible initiatives.

Maintaining the project itself — infrastructure, development, testing, documentation and distribution — is necessary for Mocha to grow and expand its social impact sustainably and transparently.

## Project status

Mocha Linux is under active development. Kernels, drivers, interfaces, tools and recovery procedures continue to be tested and documented before a stable release is published.

## Repository contents

This repository contains the public and reproducible parts of the project:

- source code and interfaces;
- build scripts and recipes;
- configurations and policies;
- technical documentation;
- visual assets;
- publishable tests, reports and maintenance tools.

Local caches, secrets, private keys, full disk images and large build artifacts remain outside GitHub.

## Licensing, compatibility and use by other distributions

Mocha Linux is composed of components from different sources. Each kernel, library, program, script, visual asset and third-party component retains its own license and the corresponding conditions for use, modification and redistribution. The presence of a file in this repository does not replace or alter the license that applies to it.

Some components maintained by the Mocha project are modified, built and validated as part of an integrated system. This includes essential system components such as the kernel, libraries, and update and recovery tools. Using them in isolation or in another distribution without evaluating their dependencies and packaging differences may cause serious incompatibilities and may even prevent the system from booting or operating correctly.

Mocha's binary repositories, update servers and other infrastructure services are sized and funded to serve the project's own users. They must not be added as package sources, mirrored or used automatically by other distributions without prior authorization. Unauthorized large-scale use may generate costs that reduce the resources available for Mocha's development and for its social mission supporting the inclusion of children with Trisomy 21.

This does not mean that we wish to prevent collaboration. On the contrary, we will be happy to speak with maintainers and developers interested in using, adapting or studying Mocha components. We can help identify the applicable license, required dependencies, the appropriate integration method and, whenever possible, a safe way to share the component without compromising the project's infrastructure.

Before integrating Mocha-specific components, redistributing its packages or using its repositories and services, please contact us at `suporte@dieseloslab.org`. This allows us to work together to avoid technical incompatibilities, licensing issues and unexpected costs for either party.

The use of third-party components distributed by Mocha remains governed exclusively by the licenses of their respective authors. Mocha trademarks, names, visual identity and original materials may also be subject to specific conditions.
